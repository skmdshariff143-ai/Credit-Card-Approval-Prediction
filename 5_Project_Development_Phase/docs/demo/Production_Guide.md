# Video Production & Editing Guide — CreditGuard AI

This production guide defines the editing timeline, visual highlights, music choices, and screen recording metrics required to assemble a premium, Apple-style product demonstration video.

---

## 🎨 Design & Theme Assets

To maintain brand consistency across slides, lower thirds, and highlights, utilize the following parameters:
- **Visual Style**: Glassmorphic elements, dark futuristic theme, drifting background blobs.
- **Color Palette**:
  - Primary Neon Cyan: `#00E5FF` (Highlights & active text elements)
  - Secondary Purple: `#6C63FF` (Accents, gauges, and graph boundaries)
  - Background Neutral: `#0A0E1A` (Dark slate backdrop)
- **Typography**: Inter or Outfit (Clean geometric sans-serif fonts)

---

## 🎵 Audio & Sound Design

### 1. Background Music Recommendations
Select high-tech, ambient corporate electronic tracks with a slow buildup:
* **Track 1**: *"Silicon Valley Corporate"* (Ambient synth pad, light digital clicks, steady rhythm)
* **Track 2**: *"Next-Gen FinTech"* (Minimalistic piano, digital pulse accents, inspirational transition at 1:30)
* **Target Volume**: Set to `-18dB` during narration, rising to `-6dB` during intro/outro animations.

### 2. Sound Effects (SFX)
* **Screen Transition**: Smooth swoosh / low-frequency wind sweep.
* **Button Click**: Soft organic "pop" or mechanical tactile click.
* **Wizard Step Slide**: Fast synthetic paper slide / sweep.
* **Score Gauge Animation**: Digital hum building to a light chime on calculation.

## 🎥 Camera Actions & Screen Recording Guide

1. **Mouse Path Smoothing**: Move the cursor in slow, deliberate, linear vectors. Do not jerk or click repeatedly.
2. **Scroll Smoothing**: Use a mouse wheel with scroll-smoothing enabled (or use PageDown/Arrow keys) to ensure webpage scrolling is cinematic.
3. **Cursor Highlight**: Encircle the pointer with a translucent yellow highlight ring (`50px` radius, 30% opacity) and set left clicks to emit red ripple waves.
4. **Key Zoom Areas**:
   - **Wizard form inputs**: Zoom in to 110% on form cards to highlight specific label inputs.
   - **LIME Explainer bars**: Zoom in on the horizontal green/red bar charts to make the text impacts readable.
   - **QR Code block**: Highlight the QR code section on the printable HTML report.
