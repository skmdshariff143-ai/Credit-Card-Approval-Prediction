Credit Card Approval Prediction

Enterprise Risk Assessment Dashboard

A production-grade Machine Learning classification platform designed to evaluate customer creditworthiness, measure delinquency probabilities, and automate card issuance decisions with explainable AI local attributions.

Start Prediction About System

Balanced Sampling Engine

Features automated class imbalance mitigation (random oversampling) to resolve severe raw target delinquency skew (92.5% Approved vs. 7.5% Rejected).

Stratified CV & Tuning

Evaluated on stratified k-fold splits ($K=5$) and optimized using GridSearchCV to guarantee model generalization on unseen bank applicants.

Local Explainability (SHAP)

Integrates a local surrogate explanation engine providing candidate risk and support attribution factors to explain decision outcomes transparently.
