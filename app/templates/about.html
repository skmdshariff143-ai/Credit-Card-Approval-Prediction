Problem Statement

Commercial banks process millions of credit card applications daily. Standard rules engines often fail to capture complex categorical feature interactions, leading to high default rates, lost revenue from rejecting creditworthy applicants, and black-box decisions violating regulatory requirements. CreditGuard AI solves this by combining machine learning pipelines with explainable scoring overlays and persistent ledgers.
